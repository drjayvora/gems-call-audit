# GEMS Central Admissions Desk – Call Audit Rubric (draft v0.1)

Each criterion is rated **2 = Met**, **1 = Partial**, **0 = Missed**, or **N/A** (doesn't apply to this call, e.g. a job-seeker call has no discovery).
Score = weighted points earned ÷ weighted points possible (N/A excluded) × 100.

| # | Criterion | Weight | Met (2) looks like |
|---|-----------|:-----:|--------------------|
| 1 | **Opening** | 1 | Greets, gives own name and "GEMS", confirms the parent by name, gives the reason for the call (enquiry, visit follow-up), checks it's a good time. |
| 2 | **Discovery** | 2 | Captures child's name, age/DOB, current school and grade, grade sought, campus/location, and what the parent actually wants (curriculum, daycare, transport, timing). Asks before pitching. |
| 3 | **Knowledge & accuracy** | 2 | Correct campus, curriculum and status (e.g. under construction). Answers what the parent asked. No overclaims, no "I'm not sure", no contradictory figures. |
| 4 | **Handling questions & objections** | 2 | Takes concerns (fees, construction, curriculum switch, competing offers, location) seriously, answers or routes them clearly, and checks the parent is satisfied. |
| 5 | **Next step secured** | 3 | Campus visit booked with a specific day, time and named counsellor, or a dated callback when a visit isn't possible. "I'll call you back" without a time is Partial. |
| 6 | **Close & recap** | 1 | Recaps what was agreed, says what happens next (address, reminder call), gives a contact, and ends warmly. |
| 7 | **Communication** | 1 | Clear and polite, matches the parent's language, listens more than talks, no long monologues, doesn't talk over the parent. |

## Red flags (not scored; any one sends the call for manager review)
- Wrong or unverifiable claims (fees, offers, numbers of schools, "every child goes to GEMS").
- Fees quoted inconsistently, or quoted when policy says not to.
- Lead data is wrong (wrong campus, not a parent, duplicate), so the CRM needs correcting.
- A hot lead at risk (competing offers, deadline) without urgency.
- A missed future lead (a sibling, a later year) not captured.
- Rude, dismissive, or pushy.

## Outcome (one per call)
`Visit booked` · `Callback scheduled` · `Callback – no date` · `Handed to campus` · `Not interested` · `Not a lead`

## CRM hygiene (checked against the call log, not the audio)
Was `call_outcome` and `intent` filled in, and does it match what happened on the call?
